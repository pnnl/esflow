Run a per-basin integrated water cycle evaluation for E3SM over the 5-year period 1985-1989, diagnosing where model biases originate across six major river basins: Amazon (3629000), Missouri (4121801), Columbia (4115200), Danube (6742900), Mekong (2969100), and Orange (1159100). Basin polygons are at "./data/sample/obs/basin_polygons.geojson".

Part 1 — Extract model fields: From ELM monthly output in "./data/sample/e3sm/" with case name "sample.v3.LR.historical" (component: elm), extract the climatological time-mean fields for precipitation (RAIN+SNOW), evapotranspiration (QVEGE+QVEGT+QSOIL), and total runoff (QRUNOFF).

Part 2 — Fetch and extract observation fields: Fetch GPCC precipitation (variable: pr, dataset: GPCCv2018), MODIS ET (fetch variable: evspsbl, dataset: MODIS; extract variable: et), and LORA runoff (variable: mrro, dataset: LORA) from ILAMB. Extract climatological time-mean fields from each.

Part 3 — Clip to basin means: For each of the six basins, clip the model P, ET, and Q fields and the observation P, ET, and runoff fields to basin-averaged values using the basin polygons.

Part 4 — Streamflow FDC metrics: Gauge metadata is at "./data/sample/obs/gauge_metadata.csv" and per-gauge observation CSVs are in "./data/sample/obs/streamflow/". Match gauges to the MOSART grid (component: mosart, variable: RIVER_DISCHARGE_OVER_LAND_LIQ), extract simulated and observed discharge for 1985-1989, and compute flow duration curve metrics (volume bias, Wasserstein distance).

Part 5 — Combine and visualise: Combine all per-basin metrics (P bias, ET bias, runoff bias, streamflow bias, water balance residual, Wasserstein distance) into a summary table. Produce a model-vs-observation bar chart comparing P, ET, and Q for each basin, and a radar chart showing the multi-variable diagnostic for each basin.

Save all outputs to "./output/task07_integrated_diagnostic".
