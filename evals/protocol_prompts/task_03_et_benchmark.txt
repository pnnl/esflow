Benchmark E3SM land model evapotranspiration against MODIS satellite observations using the 5-year climatology from 1985-1989. First, fetch the MODIS ET dataset (fetch variable: evspsbl, dataset: MODIS; extract variable: et) from ILAMB. Then extract the climatological time-mean ET from the E3SM ELM output — ET is the sum of three components: QVEGE, QVEGT, and QSOIL. The ELM data is in "./data/sample/e3sm/" with case name "sample.v3.LR.historical" (component: elm, years: 1985-1989). Also extract the time-mean ET from the MODIS observation file. Compute the spatial bias (model minus observations) and report the global mean bias and spatial correlation. Produce a map of the bias field. Save all results to "./output/task03_et_benchmark".
